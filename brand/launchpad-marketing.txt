rinsed.fun is a Solana launchpad built to make sniping, rapid bundling and copycat launches less rewarding. Our RINSE trading fee starts at 90%, steps down every three seconds toward about 1.25% over up to 27 seconds, with dynamic fees disabled. Creators can make an optional first buy of up to 2% of supply at the floor fee. Our launch flow checks for ticker reuse within the past hour, tickers already bonded here, name lookalikes, similar artwork, reused X source posts and tickers trading elsewhere with at least $10,000 in liquidity. Collected RINSE income funds buybacks and burns, with buyback budgets accumulating to at least 0.025 SOL before execution. After RINSE, Creator mode earns creator rewards, while Community mode funds hourly snapshots and claimable SOL rewards for eligible holders. Allocations use fees actually collected after protocol and any referral fees, with a share retained for platform operations; network costs are separate. Coins target 80 SOL in curve reserves before migration to Meteora DAMM v2 with permanently locked launchpad liquidity. Buybacks, burns and payouts have verifiable on-chain receipts. These checks and fees discourage abuse, but cannot eliminate bots, bundling or copies.
