reserved token-account rent repeatedly after that account already existed. Preparation now reads
actual account existence and current rent, separating permanent expenses from temporary cash needs.

income. At the live check, the first collection's expense bound was 0.00149844 SOL, with
0.00148844 SOL additionally required temporarily. Unsigned mainnet simulation confirmed collection
and closure of the wrapped-SOL account.

operating-funding check used that request and lost the verified DBC collection route. It now uses
the matched durable database row. Regression coverage also rejects reliance on changed request
details. Monetary limits, recipient checks and instruction reconstruction remain enforced.

removal of historical coins from public receipts without deleting their financial records.
