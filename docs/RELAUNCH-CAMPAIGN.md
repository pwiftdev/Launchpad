# Masked relaunch campaign

The relaunch leads with the proof page. Each day's fees, wXMR and SOL payouts, and burns are public.

**Starts only when the go-live checklist passes** ([RELAUNCH-CHECKLIST.md](RELAUNCH-CHECKLIST.md)),
including legal advice. Every post below is a draft to put in our own voice before it goes out.

## The message

**Earn from every trade. Cash out in wXMR.**

Three proof points, in this order:

1. **Daily payout totals are on the proof page.** See fees collected, wXMR and SOL paid, and $MSKD
   burned at `/proof`.
2. **Paid in wXMR on Solana.** Creators and holders claim across all their Masked coins in one go.
   SOL is also available. Direct payouts to Monero wallets are in development.
3. **Every burn has a receipt.** 0.10% of every trade buys back $MSKD and burns it; each burn gets a
   public receipt page.

Who it's for:

- **Creators:** launch in under a minute, set your split with one slider, locked forever.
- **Traders:** hold a coin and earn your share of its fees, before and after it graduates.
- **$MSKD holders:** every trade on every coin buys back and burns $MSKD.

### Hold until legal clears it

- "Your trades are public, what you earn isn't" (legal brief, question 11).
- Any wording implying returns, yield or profit from holding.
- Any claim that payouts are untraceable or anonymous. wXMR transfers are public on Solana.

## Day zero has no history, so bring some

The proof page is empty on relaunch day. Before then:

- Complete the wXMR rehearsal and show how claims appear on the proof page.
- Seed the first launches ([CREATOR-SEEDING.md](CREATOR-SEEDING.md)).
