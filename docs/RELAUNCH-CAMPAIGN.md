# Masked relaunch campaign

The relaunch leads with the proof page. We don't ask people to trust us: every day's fees, payouts
and burns are public, and the Monero treasury can be watched by anyone.

**Starts only when the go-live checklist passes** ([RELAUNCH-CHECKLIST.md](RELAUNCH-CHECKLIST.md)),
including legal advice. Every post below is a draft to put in our own voice before it goes out.

## The message

**Earn from every trade. Cash out in Monero.**

Three proof points, in this order:

1. **Every payout is on the proof page.** Fees in, payouts out, XMR bought, $MSKD burned: daily
   totals at `/proof`, with the treasury's view key so anyone can audit the Monero side.
2. **Paid in real Monero.** Creators and holders claim everything they earned across all coins in
   one go, in native XMR to their own Monero wallet, or in SOL.
3. **Every burn has a receipt.** 0.10% of every trade buys back $MSKD and burns it; each burn gets a
   public receipt page.

Who it's for:

- **Creators:** launch in under a minute, set your split with one slider, locked forever.
- **Traders:** hold a coin and earn your share of its fees, before and after it graduates.
- **$MSKD holders:** every trade on every coin buys back and burns $MSKD.

### Hold until legal clears it

- "Your trades are public, what you earn isn't" (legal brief, question 11).
- Any wording implying returns, yield or profit from holding.
- Any claim that payouts are untraceable or anonymous. Say "paid in native Monero" instead.

## Day zero has no history, so bring some

The proof page is empty on relaunch day. Before then:

- Run the 14-day stagenet rehearsal and publish its proof as a screenshot thread: "14 days of test
  payouts, every one on time."
- Seed the first launches ([CREATOR-SEEDING.md](CREATOR-SEEDING.md)), so day one has volume and the
  first daily batch has real people in it.
