# Seeding the first launches

Day one needs volume, or the proof page shows cents. We line up about 10 creators who will bring
their own audience and launch across the first week.

## Who to ask

- They already launch coins on Solana and have an audience that trades them.
- Their past launches had real volume, not just a spike in the first minutes.
- No rug history: check past coins' charts and holder lists.
- A mix: a few with large audiences, more with small but active ones.

## What we offer

- Help setting up the coin and choosing the split.
- A post from the Masked account on launch day.
- Their coin's payouts on the proof page from day one, something they can point to.

We do **not** pay creators to launch or promise them returns. If that ever changes, it is disclosed
publicly and cleared with the lawyer first.

## Ground rules for seeded launches

- No coordinated buying, no "pump at 3pm" plans.
- Creators state their split publicly; it is locked anyway.
- Spread launches across the week, not all in the first hour.

## Outreach message (draft; put it in our own voice)

> Hey [name], we're relaunching Masked: every coin splits 0.60% of every trade between its creator
> and holders, and you set the split with a slider at launch. Earnings are claimed in native Monero
> or SOL, and every payout is public on our proof page. Want to be one of the first ten launches?
> We'll help you set it up and post your launch from our account.
